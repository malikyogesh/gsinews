Naver, Nvidia & Brookfield scale up Korea's sovereign AI factory (55MW → 200MW, targeting 1GW)

Sovereign AI Data Residency Aug 18 — slightly older, still live

What happened

Naver, Nvidia, and Brookfield announced an expansion of Korea's national AI infrastructure at Naver's GAK Sejong site, from 55MW to 200MW by 2028 and a longer-term 1GW target. Nvidia is putting in $1B, Brookfield up to $9B (nonbinding). Jensen Huang's framing: "AI factories are the infrastructure nations need to compete and innovate in the intelligence era" — explicitly a sovereign-control, domestic-innovation play.
