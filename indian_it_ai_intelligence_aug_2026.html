What is actually differentiating?

TCS: domain ownership + scale. Porsche/MHP makes the automotive strategy tangible.

HCLTech: engineering depth + OpenAI + telecom infrastructure.

Wipro: turning Google Cloud's agentic AI into a delivery operating model.

Infosys: long-duration enterprise modernization + Topaz + selective AI investments.

Bottom line: The competitive battlefield is shifting from “who has the most engineers?” to “who can turn AI productivity into measurable business outcomes?” TCS currently has the strongest marquee deal signal; HCLTech has a distinctive engineering/AI infrastructure position; Wipro is pushing hyperscaler agentic AI into its operating model; Infosys is combining long-duration transformations with a focused AI portfolio. The next question is not who can build the most AI pilots — it is who can make AI economically indispensable.
