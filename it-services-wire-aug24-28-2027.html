01

Tata Consultancy Services

Automotive · Industrial ▾
Deal of the week — Aug 24

TCS agreed to acquire Porsche's captive IT and management consultancy MHP (~4,500 employees, Ludwigsburg) for an enterprise value of €320 million, alongside a linked five-year, €1.25 billion ($1.46B) partnership covering AI applications across engineering, manufacturing, operations and customer experience.

TCS will stand up a dedicated AI Mobility Centre of Excellence for Porsche, focused on industrializing AI use cases across the product and value chain. The deal deepens TCS's German and broader European footprint and signals a shift toward "acquire-and-transform" plays rather than pure services contracts.

Tech & partnership context — past month

  • Launched the TCS Autonomous Engineering Lab Powered by NVIDIA at its Bengaluru Global Axis campus (mid-July) to move Industrial AI from pilot to production for manufacturers and mobility firms.
  • Earlier launched Rapid Outcome AI, an NVIDIA-built platform to speed AI from experimentation to production, with initial focus on manufacturing, banking and retail.
  • Diamond Sponsor at NVIDIA GTC 2026, showcasing Physical AI, Agentic AI and real-time AI systems.
  • Deepened its Google Cloud partnership (April) around AI-native autonomous enterprises.
02

HCLTech

Telecom · Sovereign AI ▾
M&A activity — past month

Completed acquisition of HPE's Telco Solutions business (Aug 3), strengthening AI-led telecom capabilities with expanded IP, engineering talent and global delivery capacity for Communications Service Providers — HCLTech's second HPE telecom deal after the 2024 CTG acquisition.

Signed a $1.14 billion mega-deal with a Europe-headquartered Fortune Global 50 company for an AI-driven operating model spanning its Global Digital Workplace and Enterprise Networks (July 3), ending a three-year mega-deal drought — shares jumped ~6% on the news.

  • Signed a partnership with Guardian (July 16) for AI-led modernization of technology and operations.
  • Completed acquisition of the Jaspersoft business unit (July 2) and acquired wealth-management consultancy Finergic (January).

Tech & AI infrastructure

  • Announced a Rs 14,257-crore AI Data Center in Bhubaneswar with Sarvam and the Government of Odisha (July 24) — part of India's sovereign AI push — alongside a new Global Technology Center housing 5,000 people.
  • Launched a Physical AI Innovation Lab with NVIDIA in Santa Clara for cognitive robotics and industrial AI applications.
  • Named a Leader in Gartner's 2026 Magic Quadrant for Public Cloud Optimization and Transformation Services.
  • Earlier this year: launched a Gemini Enterprise Business Unit with Google, and AI Force 2.0 for agentic AI.
03

Wipro

Agentic AI · Google Cloud ▾
Deal of the week — Aug 27

Wipro announced an expanded partnership with Google Cloud to accelerate Gemini Enterprise and agentic AI adoption — equipping over 10,000 AI-certified specialists (including 1,500 "Forward Deployed Engineers") and launching the LIFT (Launch-Ignite-Flywheel-Transform) framework. Wipro's US ADRs jumped as much as 7% pre-market on the news.

LIFT integrates Gemini Enterprise with Wipro's own WINGS and WEGA AI delivery platforms, moving clients from task automation to autonomous, multi-step agentic workflows.

Other recent wins & partnerships

  • Multi-year AI-enabled IT modernization deal with Dutch telecom Odido.
  • GenAI-led "IT 2.0" transformation partnership with HanesBrands.
  • Recognized as a Services-as-Software Star and Market Leader in HFS Horizons: Agentic Services 2026; announced a new AI-Native Business & Platforms Unit and a strategic collaboration with Harness for AI-native software delivery.
  • Expanded CrowdStrike partnership for AI-powered "CyberShield MDR" managed security.
04

Infosys

Retail · BFSI ▾
Deal of the week — Aug 6

Infosys signed a 10-year strategic agreement with US footwear maker Crocs to modernize core business and technology systems using AI, covering operations in more than 85 countries — aimed at unifying data, eliminating silos and enabling real-time decision-making. Financial terms undisclosed.

Other recent wins & partnerships

  • Expanded its long-standing collaboration with Finland's Metsä Group (Aug 5) for AI-led IT transformation, built around Infosys Topaz Fabric, its agentic services suite.
  • Infosys Finacle won a multi-country transformation deal with Investec, migrating South Africa, UK, Mauritius and Channel Islands operations to Finacle on Microsoft Azure.
  • Partnership with Anthropic to advance AI in regulated industries (banking, healthcare).
  • Recognized as a leader in AI across 12 industry ratings; reported $4.8B in large deal TCV in a recent quarter, 57% net new.

Positioning, Read Plainly

What the moves signal

TCS

Leaning into large, capital-intensive strategic deals bundled with AI transformation rather than pure services contracts — a shift toward "acquire + transform" plays in automotive and industrial.

HCLTech

The most acquisitive of the four this year (HPE Telco, Jaspersoft, Finergic), doubling down on sovereign AI infrastructure in India alongside its long-running NVIDIA and telecom engineering strength.

Wipro

Had the most stock-moving news this specific week — its Google Cloud/Gemini expansion — signaling a bet on becoming the go-to agentic-AI integrator on Google's stack, distinct from TCS and HCLTech's NVIDIA-centric physical-AI framing.

Infosys

Steady cadence of large, long-duration client wins, leaning on its Topaz/Finacle product suites plus a notable Anthropic partnership for regulated industries.
